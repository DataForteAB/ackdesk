# Ackdesk for Confluence

Who has read the policy — on the page, in Confluence search, and in one report.

An Atlassian Forge app by **DataForte AB**, available on the Atlassian Marketplace.

## What it does

- Ask everyone, a group, or named people to confirm they have read a Confluence page.
  Each person clicks once; the signature records who, when, and which version they read.
- A new page version reopens the round and clears the signatures, because a signature against
  version 3 is not evidence that anyone read version 7.
- Signatures can be given a shelf life per space. A daily sweep reopens the round when the
  period lapses, even on pages nobody has opened for a year.
- The state is written to a Confluence label, so `label = "ack-outstanding"` finds every page
  still waiting, in Confluence's own search.
- One report shows where the company stands: overall coverage, which spaces are behind, what has
  been waiting longest, and each person's own outstanding reading list.
- Runs entirely on Atlassian Forge with **no external calls** — nothing about your pages or
  people leaves Atlassian, and there is no server to run.
