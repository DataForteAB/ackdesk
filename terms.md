## What the app is, and is not

Ackdesk records who approved a page and when, and marks an approval as no longer current when the
page changes or a review period lapses. It is a record-keeping and workflow aid. It is **not** a
legal signature service, and it does not by itself make a page compliant with any standard or
regulation. You are responsible for deciding what your organisation's approvals mean.

## Availability

The app runs on Atlassian Forge and is available as Atlassian's platform is available. Approval
decisions are recorded when Confluence delivers the corresponding event; where Atlassian delays or
drops an event, state may lag until the next page open or the daily sweep.
