## Before you write

Most questions fall into one of these:

**Where do I find it on a page?** Ackdesk appears next to the page byline, under the title. It
shows the current state, and clicking it opens the reviewers and the actions.

**Nothing happens when I ask for approval.** Approvals are recorded only while the subscription is
active. If the licence lapsed, existing approvals stay visible but new decisions are not recorded.

**An approved page went back to "In review".** That is deliberate: someone edited the page. The
approval described the previous version, so it was cleared. A space admin can switch this off in
*Space settings → Approvals*.

**An approval expired on its own.** The space has a review-by period. Clear it in the space
settings if approvals should last indefinitely.

**Searching for approved pages.** The state is mirrored onto a page label. Search Confluence for
`label = "approval-approved"` or `label = "approval-expired"`. The prefix can be changed in the
site settings.

**Who can approve?** Only the people listed as reviewers on that request. Everyone else sees the
state but not the buttons.

## Reporting a problem

Include the site URL, the page, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots help. Security
issues: write to hello@dataforteab.com with "SECURITY" in the subject; we reply within two business
days.
